Before you start — rules that apply every week

The weekly content may change. What is shown in class and how a workshop runs can be adjusted during the term — check the week before you rely on it. The assessment does not change. The 24 sections, the four marking criteria and the deadline are fixed, and anything that did change would be announced, not quietly edited.

Nothing is handed in weekly. There is one deck, set up in week 1 from the structure on the hand-in page, shared with tutors, and filled in a section at a time. The "sections you fill in" box on each sheet is the part of that deck the workshop produces.

Pair work, not group work. Every workshop has one swap in it. It is a checking step, never a shared deliverable — the portfolio is individual, and the swap exists to test whether the work reads without its author standing next to it. Nothing a student makes depends on a partner turning up.
