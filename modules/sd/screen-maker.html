Screen maker

The paper organisms, on screen. Click an organism on the left to add it, or drag it onto the screen. It snaps to the rows: put it on any row you like, leave rows empty, drag it up or down. Click any words on the screen to change them. Work at phone or desktop size. Set up the master once with what every screen shares, such as the bars at the top and bottom. Make one screen for each step of your flow, then copy each one into your slides or print them all for B5. See every organism at full size on Organisms.

This is the example: the missed bin journey, one screen per step. Try anything: your own screens are kept safe, and nothing here is saved over them.
