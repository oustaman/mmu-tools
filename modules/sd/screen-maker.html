Screen maker

The paper organisms, on screen. Click an organism to add it under the one you selected, or drag it into place. It snaps into whole rows, top bars stay at the top and a tab bar at the bottom, and a screen is full at 14. Nothing fits? Use a blank piece of the height you need and name it. An overlay, such as a dialog or the keyboard, lies over the screen instead of taking rows. Every organism has the code printed on its paper piece, so a screen made on paper can be rebuilt here code by code, or the other way round. Make one screen for each step of your flow, then print them or save them as a PDF for B5.
