How it works

  • Add: click an organism on the left, or drag it onto a row. It snaps to the rows, and you can leave rows empty.
  • Words: click any words on a screen and type.
  • Move: drag a piece, or use the buttons beside it. With the keyboard: Tab to a piece, then the arrow keys; Delete removes it, Enter edits its words.
  • Master: what you put on the first tile appears on every screen, such as the bars at the top and bottom.
  • The canvas: scroll to move around, pinch or Ctrl/⌘ and scroll to zoom, and drag the empty space to pan. Hold Space and drag from anywhere.
  • Notes: press Annotate, click on or beside a screen to drop a number, and say what it points at. Copy as a picture then brings the screen, the numbers and the notes together.
  • Save: your screens stay in this browser. Press Save to keep them as text, and paste it back to carry on anywhere.
  • Your deck: make one screen for each step of your flow, then Copy as a picture into your slides, or print the set for B5.
