Screen maker

The paper organisms, on screen. Open a family on the left and click an organism to add it, or drag it onto the screen. It snaps to the rows: put it on any row you like, leave rows empty, drag it up or down. Click any words on the screen to change them. Set up the master once with what every screen shares, such as the app bar and tab bar. Make one screen for each step of your flow, then copy each one into your slides or print them all for B5. See every organism at full size on Organisms.

Organisms

Your screens stay in this browser, on this computer. Copy them into your slides, or print them, before you close it. The example is the missed bin journey from the Flow maker, one screen per step.
