Before and after

Real services, walked step by step, then improved. Each case shows what is wrong now — measured, not described — the same job redesigned, the A3, A4 and A5 behind the redesign, and what a marker would credit and where it goes in your deck. They are not portfolios to copy. They show the thinking that earns marks, on something real.

  1. Now — every screen, with what goes wrong numbered on it.
  2. Measured — words, taps, fields, contrast: the counts behind each problem.
  3. Improved — the same job, redesigned, with each decision explained.
  4. The decisions — the redesign’s own A3, A4 and A5.
  5. Counted — before against after, and what happened to every step.
  6. Why it is better — what a marker credits, and which deck section it belongs in.
