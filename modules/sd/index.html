Screen Design 2026–27

One journey, made better, and a record of why it looks like that. You pick one thing a person is trying to get done, find out how they do it now and count the steps, then design a shorter, clearer version and write down every choice as you make it. It all goes in one slide deck, filled in a section or two each week and handed in as one PDF: 100% of the mark, with no exam, presentation or group work.
