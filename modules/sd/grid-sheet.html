This grid: 4 columns of 14.5 mm · gutters 4 mm · margins 5 mm · screen 80 mm wide. On a real phone that is about 4 columns, 16 px gutters and 16 px margins — numbers you can write straight into A3.

Blocks — cut along the dashed lines

Each block is 1, 2, 3 or 4 columns wide, so it either fits the grid or plainly does not. You will not need all of them. The blank ones are for anything your screen has that is not here.
