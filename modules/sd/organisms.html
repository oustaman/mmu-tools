Organisms

Every organism in the set, at full size and with real words. An organism does one job on a screen: a question with its answers, a search with its filters. On a phone every one is 375 px wide, as wide as the screen, and a whole number of 56 px rows tall, so any of them fits under any other. On desktop the same rows run across 12 columns, and an organism takes as many columns as you give it. The code is the one printed on its paper piece. Put them together in the Screen maker, or print them from the paper kit.
