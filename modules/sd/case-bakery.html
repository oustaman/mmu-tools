Ordering a celebration cake

Boulangerie Vauclerc, 7 Mallow Street, Castlegate — a made-up bakery, drawn from the way many small food businesses take orders today: a website that is only a front door, and everything else in a message thread. The social page and the messages are drawn generically, and every name and number is invented.

WhoSomeone ordering a birthday cake for 12 people, to collect at the weekend — on a phone, in a lunch break.
GoalKnow the price, pick the cake, choose when to collect it, and be sure it is booked.
Why this caseIt is the own-business route several of you are taking: chat now, one booking flow after — and the owner can still confirm each order by hand.

A front door you have to get past

The customer wants a birthday cake for 12 people on Saturday. First, the website.

  1. 1Music, on its ownAn accordion plays as the page opens — in a lunch break, at a desk, on a bus. The mute is a small pale label in the corner.
  2. 2A splash page that says “ENTER”One extra tap before anything at all, in pale gold letters on a photo — as low as 1.9:1.
  3. 3“Accept all”, and no other buttonThe cookie banner covers half the screen and offers only agreement. That is not a choice — and the UK regulator expects refusing to be as easy as accepting.

The redesign assumes the bakery can take card payments online, or offer pay-in-shop only. A booking tool the owner checks by hand is enough; nothing here needs a custom system.

What a marker would credit, and where it goes in your deck

The before is real evidence, even when it is a chatA message thread, counted — messages, hours, places to look — is a “before” as strong as any website.
The person is keptThe owner still confirms each order. Designing around how a small business actually works is user-centred too.
Accessibility is specificText prices instead of a photo, crossed-out days explained in words, contrast raised from 2.2:1 to 7.7:1.
An ethics answer you can point atA required allergy question; no bank details in chats.
The look is kept, and made legibleThe warmth survives in the colours and the serif; the numbers make it consistent.
It states its limitsThe bakery is made up, the counts come from the drawn thread, and the payment assumption is written down.

Boulangerie Vauclerc, its address and phone number are invented; 01632 960 is a range reserved for drama and examples. The social page and messages are drawn generically, not copied from any service.
