A grid in seven numbers

One slide per number, each with a screen you can change. On the last slide you set your own.

  • Columns — the strips everything lines up to
  • Gutter — the gap between columns
  • Margin — screen edge to content
  • Max width — where the content stops growing
  • Breakpoints — where the layout changes
  • Spacing scale — the only gaps you allow

The examples use Example 2’s numbers. Move with ← →; Present for the projector.

1 of 6 · Columns

Columns

  • Equal vertical strips. Every box starts on one and ends on another.
  • Boxes take whole columns: the form takes 6 of 8.
  • Bigger screens, more columns: 8 · 6 · 4.

Change the columns. Then try 16 on Mobile.

2 of 6 · Gutter

Gutter

  • The empty gap between columns.
  • Too small and boxes touch; too big and the page falls apart.

Example 2: 24 px, 16 on mobile. Try 0, then 48.

3 of 6 · Margin

Margin

  • From the screen edge to the content, on both sides.
  • On a phone it costs content, so keep it small.

Example 2: 40 · 32 · 16 px. Try 0 on mobile.

4 of 6 · Max width

Max width

  • Where the content stops getting wider.
  • Without it, lines grow too long to read.

Example 2: 960 px. Turn it off and widen the screen.

5 of 6 · Breakpoints

Breakpoints

  • Screen widths where the numbers change.
  • Example 2: 1024 desktop · 768 tablet · 375 phone.
  • List only the sizes you design.

Drag the width and watch the columns switch.

6 of 6 · Spacing scale

Spacing scale

  • Columns handle space across; the scale handles the gaps down the page.
  • Pick a few sizes. Every gap uses one.

Example 2: 4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 40 · 64. Press Any gap.
