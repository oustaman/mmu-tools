What a contrast ratio means

Contrast is how different a text colour is from its background, written as a ratio. 1:1 is text the same colour as its background — invisible. 21:1 is black on white — the most there is.

Aa1.5:1fails
Aa2.2:1fails
Aa3:1large text only
Aa4.5:1the minimum
Aa7:1better
Aa21:1the most
  • Aim for 4.5:1 or more for all ordinary text — body text, labels, links and the words on buttons. This is the baseline (WCAG 2.2, success criterion 1.4.3, level AA).
  • 3:1 is enough only for large text (24px, or about 19px bold) and for icons, borders and focus outlines.
  • 7:1 or more is better (level AAA) — worth aiming for small text, and for people reading outdoors or with low vision.
  • “8 pieces of text below 4.5:1” means eight bits of wording on that page that fail the minimum. Check any pair with the WebAIM contrast checker, and write the ratio into A5.
