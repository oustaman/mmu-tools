How it is marked

      Reproduced verbatim from the assessment brief · CAF Phase 2

    
One overall mark for the portfolio as a whole. The four criteria are not
      weighted separately — the brief says so, and it means a strong prototype does not offset a
      rationale that evidences nothing.

    

      

    

    

  


  

    
The one thing that changes your grade

    
In Part C, every section has to quote a number you wrote in Part A. That is it. It is the
      difference between a pass and a good mark, and it takes no extra work — the numbers are
      already in your spec sheet.

    ✗  "I chose a warm palette because it feels friendly."

      ✓  "I changed body text from #8B8382 to #56504F because the first pair was 2.9:1 and failed AA."
  


  

    
Using AI is fine — just log it

    
Figma AI, image generators, anything. Nobody is marking whether you used them. Fill in
      Part A §7: which tool, what it made, what you changed, why. One line per use.

The 24 sections, the four marking criteria and the deadline are fixed, and anything that did change would be announced, not quietly edited.
