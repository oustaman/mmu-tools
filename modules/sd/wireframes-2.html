Screen 3 of 6

A big block earns its rows

A date picker takes six of the fourteen rows. Here that is right: the day is the whole point of the screen. In the finished screen, full days stay visible and crossed out, so nobody thinks they were overlooked.

    Screen 4 of 6

    A picture box can hold a decision

    “Happy 30th Sam”, shown as it will be piped, so a misspelt name is caught here and not on Friday. And the allergy question is asked every time: it cannot be skipped, because it is the one answer that can hurt someone.

      The person needs to…

      …choose

      One answer, or several? Round marks for one, square for several. Only two answers? Yes or no is quicker than a list.

      The person needs to…

      …give you something

      Ask for as little as you can. Every box is work for them. Name the data you chose not to collect: that is a ready answer for C6.

      The person needs to…

      …find something

      A few things? Just list them. Search earns its place when there are too many to look through.

      The person needs to…

      …look at one thing, or compare a few

      The person needs to…

      …do something bigger

      The biggest blocks take six rows, nearly half a phone screen. If one of them is the screen’s job, let it have the room, and take everything else away.

      The person needs to…

      …get around

      A tab bar is for apps with several main parts. A journey with a beginning and an end usually wants a way back and a progress bar instead.

      The service answers back · this is today’s real subject

      Empty, loading and failed are screens too

      For every screen on your list, ask: what does it look like empty, loading, and when it fails? This usually adds one or two screens.

      The odd ones out

      Overlays lie over a screen

      OVL-01 Dialog

      OVL-02 Bottom sheet

      OVL-03 Keyboard

      OVL-04 Toast

      They take no rows, and you get one at a time. A dialog stops everything, so save it for what cannot be undone. In a wireframe it needs a screen of its own: duplicate the screen, and add the overlay.

      When the set runs out

      Nothing fits? Write a note

      The set will not have everything. A camera view, a QR scanner, a chart: put a note where it would go, the height it would take, and write what it would do. On paper it is a box to draw in. There is one for each height: OWN-01 is one row, OWN-06 is six.

      That is a real wireframe decision. You have said what is there and how much room it takes. You do not have to be able to draw it.

      Desktop · the same rows, across 12 columns

      On a big screen, things go side by side

      The same words as “Which cake?”, adapted and not stretched: what goes beside what?

      Only needed if your journey is used on a big screen. A better flow is worth more than more sizes.
