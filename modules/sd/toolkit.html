Toolkit

Tools and websites for the fundamentals — layout, type, colour, access — and for collecting the exact numbers your deck asks for. Each card says which section of the deck it helps you fill. Most are free; none is required. Use whatever you are comfortable with.

Still choosing a theme? Theme ideas gives you a ready-made one at the press of a button — someone specific, one goal, how people do it now and the screens you would need — to take as it is or change.

Collect the codes from any website — no install
  1. In Chrome or Edge, right-click the page and choose Inspect.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu in the panel → More tools → CSS Overview → Capture overview.
  3. You get every colour on the page, every typeface and font size, and a list of contrast problems — click any one to copy its code.
  4. Paste what you collected into A4 and A5, and say where it came from.
