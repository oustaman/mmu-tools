Flow maker

Your journey as a slide for B4, made in two steps. Week 5: list what a person does now, step by step, and mark where they get stuck. Week 7: make your version from a copy of it. Remove steps, merge them, move them and add screens, and the tool keeps track of what happened to each one. That record is your D2.

One row for everything a person does or waits for, in order, from the first search to getting what they came for. If a row is a yes/no question, pick Yes/no question and say what happens if the answer is no. Press ✗ stuck wherever people go wrong, and say why. These are the problems your version has to fix.

    1. Press . You get a copy to change.
    2. Work through it with the problems in front of you. ✕ removes a step, merge ↓ joins a step to the next one, and ↑ ↓ moves a step. Rename a step to say what your screen does, and + Add a screen for anything new.
    3. The numbers under each row show which steps from now it came from. The slide labels every old step kept, merged, moved, changed or removed.

      Every step from now, and what happened to it in your version. The tool fills in the middle column. You write the why: that is what makes D2 evidence rather than a list. Then Copy “what happened” for D2.
