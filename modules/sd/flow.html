How to write a flow
Open the council's bins page — websiteA step. Anything after — is the tool or screen, written smaller underneath.
Will you create an account?A decision: end the line with ? and give it another way out on the next line. The journey carries on underneath as “yes”. A question with no other way out — “Which bin?” — is drawn as an ordinary screen.
no: phone the contact centre !The other way out of a decision: start the line with spaces. End it with ! if people get stuck there.
no: come back after 5pm -> 3A way out that sends people back to step 3.
~ 2 working daysA wait. It is not a step, but it is counted.
! Error, form clearedA step where people get stuck: start the line with !.
The first and last steps are drawn rounded: where people come in, and where they get what they came for. Steps are numbered for you, so they match A2 and D1.
