        How it works

        • Week 5, Now: one row for everything a person does or waits for, in order. Press ✗ wherever people go wrong, and say why. Those are the problems your version has to fix.
        • A question is for when the answer changes where people go next. Give each answer a name and say where it goes. A new box puts one under the question, and arrows can join up again later.
        • Colours mean whatever you say they mean. Pick one, say what it means, and it joins the key on the slide.
        • Move a box by dragging it. The arrows follow.
        • Week 7, Your version: press Start from my “now” for a copy to change. Remove, merge, move and rename steps, and add screens. Each step shows which steps from now it came from.
        • What happened: the tool labels every old step kept, merged, moved or removed, in a table you can copy. That is your D2; you write why.
        • The diagram follows the tab, and it is what you copy. Click any box, diamond, arrow or line of words: its setting lights up on the right, and if it is words you can type straight away. Scroll to move around, pinch or Ctrl/⌘ and scroll to zoom.
        • Prefer paper? Boxes and arrows, left to right, photographed into B4.
