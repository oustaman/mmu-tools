      How it works

      • Week 5, Now: one row for everything a person does or waits for, in order. Press ✗ wherever people go wrong, and say why. Those are the problems your version has to fix.
      • A yes/no question is only for when the answer changes where people go next. Say what happens on “no”.
      • Week 7, Your version: press Start from my “now” for a copy to change. Remove, merge, move and rename steps, and add screens. The chips under each row show which steps from now it came from.
      • What happened: the tool labels every old step kept, merged, moved or removed. That is your D2; you write why.
      • The canvas: click a box to open its step. Scroll to move around, pinch or Ctrl/⌘ and scroll to zoom.
      • Prefer paper? Boxes and arrows, left to right, photographed into B4.

      Your work stays in this browser, on this computer: download the slide and copy both lists before you close it. New to flows? How flows work has every option, with real examples.
