      How it works

      • Week 5, Now: one row for everything a person does or waits for, in order. Press ✗ wherever people go wrong, and say why. Those are the problems your version has to fix.
      • A yes/no question is only for when the answer changes where people go next. Say what happens on “no”.
      • Week 7, Your version: press Start from my “now” for a copy to change. Remove, merge, move and rename steps, and add screens. Each step shows which steps from now it came from.
      • What happened: the tool labels every old step kept, merged, moved or removed. That is your D2; you write why.
      • The diagram follows the tab, and it is what you download. Click any box, diamond or line of words to select it and type. Its settings open on the left. Scroll to move around, pinch or Ctrl/⌘ and scroll to zoom.
      • Prefer paper? Boxes and arrows, left to right, photographed into B4.
