All pages

Everything on this site, on one page. From any page, All pages at the top brings you back here.

The submission needs to show

  1. A clickable prototype: the screens, at one or more sizes. Add clicks to your PowerPoint and you’re done.
  2. Process work: sketches, wireframes, mood board and style tiles. Sketches on paper or in design software: both count. The appendices count here too.
  3. A rationale of 500–700 words, covering layout, type, colour, users, accessibility, AI, one ethics or sustainability point and one cross-cultural point.
