What to hand in

This is the complete list. Nothing else is required.

The submission needs to show

  1. A clickable prototype: the screens, at one or more sizes. Add clicks to your PowerPoint and you’re done.
  2. Process work: sketches, wireframes, mood board and style tiles. Sketches on paper or in design software: both count. The appendices count here too.
  3. A rationale of 500–700 words, covering layout, type, colour, users, accessibility, AI, one ethics or sustainability point and one cross-cultural point.

Set up your deck Once, in week 1: a shared folder, one deck inside it, and your student ID on the cover. Then a section or two every week.
  1. In your university OneDrive, make a folder called exactly Screen Design. Everything you make goes in it: the deck, photos of paper work, anything you want a tutor to see.
  2. Share it with your tutors and the teaching staff, and leave it shared. Nobody looks in it unless you ask; when you want feedback or help, say so, and that is where we look.
  3. Inside it, a PowerPoint deck called screen-design-portfolio (Google Slides is fine too), with a slide for each section: copy the cards below, one per slide.
  4. Your student ID goes on the cover, and your name goes nowhere: not the filename, a header or a footer, because marking is anonymous. If your name slips in, nothing happens.
  5. Photograph paper work and paste it into the deck the same day. From week 8, every screen gets its own slide; in week 10 you link them, and that is your prototype.
1 One rule for every piece of text: it fits on one slide No caption, answer or paragraph runs past the slide it is on. Nothing else is counted, except the written rationale in Part C, which comes to about 500–700 words in all: the brief's one strict limit. There is no limit on the number of slides.

Hand in

One PDF of your deck, uploaded to Moodle by . Late work is subject to the standard MMU late penalty policy.

  1. Before you export, duplicate the Screen Design folder. Exports go wrong, and you do not want to rebuild.
  2. Export the deck as a PDF, open the PDF, and click every link in it: a link that works in the editor can vanish in the export, and the PDF is what is marked.
  3. Upload that one file to Moodle.

Written feedback comes back within four weeks of the deadline, through Moodle and Turnitin. Grades are provisional until the Assessment Board confirms them, and with anonymous marking the grade and the written feedback can arrive at different times. How it is marked.
